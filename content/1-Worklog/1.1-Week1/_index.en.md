The first week focuses on the First Cloud AI Journey internship program at AWS Vietnam and establishing a learning direction. I begin with cloud computing concepts before studying individual services.

## Weekly objectives

- Understand the learning direction, documentation sources and worklog structure.
- Distinguish service models, Regions and Availability Zones, and identify initial account and cost considerations.

## Weekly reflection

I developed an initial picture of how AWS organizes infrastructure and what to consider before using services. Account protection and cost management should be considered from the start alongside technical learning.

## Point to clarify

A budget alert does not mean that all resources automatically stop at the threshold. Tracking, notifications and separately configured automated actions serve different purposes.
