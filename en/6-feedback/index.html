Feedback and suggestions

My name is Lê Hoàng Tùng, a student in class K68CNTTA at Vietnam National University of Agriculture, participating in the First Cloud AI Journey program. During the internship, I studied AWS services and was responsible for the EC2 and RDS components of CloudDocs. This helped me connect what I had learned with deploying and operating a practical application.

Assessment by aspect

1. Internship environment

The program enabled me to study independently using Cloud Journey materials and relate concepts to project work. A topic-based learning path gave me clearer direction while helping me develop independent research habits and organize my study time.

2. Mentorship and support

Documentation and community discussions gave me additional ways to approach unfamiliar topics. I found that preparing configuration details, error messages and attempted steps made discussions more effective.

3. Relevance to Information Technology

The internship topics connected with networking, databases and web development studied at university. Participating in a project helped me understand how these concepts work together in a system and recognize the importance of communication and coordination between team members.

4. Skills with the clearest progress

  • Better understanding of the roles of EC2 and RDS in the CloudDocs architecture.
  • Practice in checking server configurations, database connection details and network access permissions.
  • Coordinating with team members to compare infrastructure and application configurations.

5. Community culture

Shared materials and meetup activities exposed me to further learning and working experiences with AWS. I found the community a suitable place for newcomers to ask questions, discuss difficulties and learn from more experienced members.

6. Internship conditions

Online resources gave me flexibility to study and look up information as needed. Effective learning required setting aside time for practice, recording results and discussing difficulties. Using AWS resources also helped me become more aware of cost tracking and learning-environment management.

Feedback questions and suggestions

What satisfied me most?

I was most satisfied with the opportunity to connect what I had learned with a concrete project rather than studying each topic in isolation. Participating in CloudDocs helped me develop responsibility, coordinate with the team and recognize skills I need to improve.

What could the program improve?

  • Add step-by-step exercises with expected outcomes and common errors so newcomers can check their work.
  • Offer more discussions with mentors about challenging topics and approaches to difficulties.
  • Explain how to maintain a worklog, select useful resources and collect evidence from the beginning of the internship.

Would I recommend the program to friends?

Yes. I would recommend it to friends who want to learn AWS and are willing to take an active role in learning. Combining documentation with a concrete project helps learners connect concepts with practical work.

Additional suggestions

  • Organize short exchanges between learners to share study and teamwork experiences.
  • Add career-orientation discussions to help students understand Cloud roles and the skills they require.
  • Provide general guidance on cost management, handover and resource cleanup at the end of exercises or projects.

Would I like to stay involved with the program?

Yes. I would like to continue learning through Cloud Journey, participate in suitable community activities and apply my knowledge to further exercises and projects. I also hope to share the experience I gain so that other learners and I can progress together.

Acknowledgments

I thank the mentors and the First Cloud AI Journey community for sharing their knowledge and experience during the internship. Learning and participating in CloudDocs helped me clarify my direction and recognize the skills I need to keep developing.

Lê Hoàng Tùng
